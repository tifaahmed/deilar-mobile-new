# CLAUDE.md — Deilar mobile app (`deilar-mobile-new`)

Read `CLAUDE_GUIDE.md` for the architecture. This app is a CLIENT of `../ibusiness_medical` (all rules and numbers live there) and shares its product contract with `../deilar` (the website).

## Trial cards, subscriptions & relatives — THE CONTRACT (same text in all three projects)

> Deilar is three projects: `ibusiness_medical` (Laravel backend — owns every rule and every number), `deilar` (the website) and `deilar-mobile-new` (the app). The website and the app are CLIENTS: they relay what a member typed and DRAW what the backend answers. If you change a rule, change it in `ibusiness_medical` first and update this section in all three files in the same change.

1. **Free trial card.** A member makes one from the app or the website with ONLY: the phone they signed in with (proved by the SMS code), a **name** (no national ID). It works immediately, `plan = trial`, and expires after `membership_trial_months` (default **1 month**; admin setting). It holds the OWNER ONLY — never a relative.
2. **One trial per phone, ever.** Enforced by the backend: `trial_claims` (DB unique index on `phone` — survives deleted cards) and any other account with the same phone that holds a card. The national ID is NOT asked for any more (it can be added later in the profile). A second device signs into the SAME account (login is by phone) so it just sees the same card; another account row with the same phone is refused (`reason`: `phone_taken`, `already_has_card`). Never weaken this, never add a client-side "retry with another phone".
3. **The backend decides what a card IS.** `Membership::state()` → `pending | trial | trial_expired | active | expired | inactive`; clients get it as `status` (`MembershipStatus::forCard` for anyone scanning, `forMember` for the signed-in owner). Clients NEVER compute expiry, price, "can add relatives" or the profile percentage themselves.
4. **Expired card = a big RED sign.** Whenever a card is scanned or opened (QR page, the website's `/membership/{number}`, the app's card screen) and `status.expired` is true (a trial that ended OR a paid card that lapsed) the page leads with a large red "CARD EXPIRED" sign and three options: **Subscribe now with Paymob** (only if `status.can_pay_online`; hidden when the price is 0 or Paymob is unset), **Call customer service** (`tel:` the phone), **Message customer service** (WhatsApp). Contacts come from `status.contact` (admin settings) — never hard-code a number. A running trial shows a small amber banner with the days left. An expired card earns NO member price (`Membership::isCurrent()`).
5. **Paying for a card.** Paymob (online, automatic — the callback activates the card), or through customer services: **cash on delivery**, **Vodafone Cash**, **Instapay** (the last two with a receipt upload). Price and months are admin settings (`membership_price`, `membership_months`); 0 price = not sold online. A receipt is a CLAIM: it never extends a card by itself — an admin confirms it at `/admin/subscription`. Only `ActivateSubscription` adds card time (a verified Paymob callback, or an admin pressing confirm); it is idempotent, so a repeated callback or click can never grant two periods. A trial becomes `full` and is paid from the day of payment. **Which methods are SHOWN is an admin switch** (`/admin/setting`, `App\Support\PaymentMethods`): `payment_paymob_card_enabled` and `payment_paymob_wallet_enabled` (Paymob by bank card / by mobile wallet such as Vodafone Cash — two separate buttons, each its own Paymob integration: `PAYMOB_CARD_INTEGRATION_ID`, `PAYMOB_WALLET_INTEGRATION_ID`; clients send `paymob_channel` = `card`|`wallet`; both default on), `payment_cod_enabled` (cash on delivery, default off), `payment_wallet_enabled` (wallet transfer / Vodafone Cash with receipt, default off), `payment_instapay_enabled` (default off). Orders and subscriptions are both gated by it and the backend refuses a hidden method; clients draw only `payment_methods` (`/mobile/shop`, `/partner/shop-settings`) and `status.payment_methods` — never a hard-coded list.
6. **Paymob callbacks** are public and trusted ONLY through the HMAC; a paid amount that is not the price is never accepted; every HTTP exchange is stored verbatim with timing in `order_payment_logs` (shared with orders). Subscription references are `SUB<id>A<attempt>`, order codes are `DL-XXXXXXXX` — never mix them.
7. **Relatives are MEMBERS (one table, not two).** A relative is a `memberships` row with `member_type = 'relative'`, `parent_membership_id` (the PRIMARY card — never another relative) and `relationship`, plus its own `users` row (name, phone, date of birth live there, as for any member). The old `family_members` table is RETIRED: its rows were copied by migration `2026_10_29_100000_add_relatives_to_memberships` (`legacy_family_member_id` keeps the link; a phone / e-mail / National ID already held by someone else was NOT copied and sits in `legacy_notes`, the closest primary keeps it). The table itself is dropped in a LATER release, after the copy is confirmed in production. Rules (model `Membership::guardRelative` + `App\Actions\Membership\{CreateRelative,UpdateRelative}` — the only way to make or edit one): a relative needs a relation and a primary parent; **at least one of phone / date of birth / National ID** for a member-added relative (all three allowed; rows copied from the old table are exempt) — but **an ADMIN may file or edit a relative with the NAME alone** (`CreateRelative` with `added_by = admin`, `UpdateRelative(..., byAdmin: true)`, `Membership::$skipIdentityCheck`; a repeated phone / National ID is still refused); phone, e-mail and National ID are unique like any member's; **no subscription of its own** (`plan = 'relative'`, no expiry — `state()`/`isCurrent()` follow the primary); address, partner, company and sales are inherited from the primary; the number is six digits like every new member (`Membership::newNumber()`; older numbers are untouched); a **son or daughter may be typed with the FIRST name only** (the primary's name is appended), every other relation needs the full name; **deleting a primary deletes its relatives**; a relative signs in only with THEIR OWN card number (`CardLoginController`; the code goes to the phone on that card, or they add their own once) — phone sign-in (`MemberOtp::findMember`) stays primary-only. A card holds at most **15 first-degree relatives** (wife, husband, son, daughter, father, mother — siblings are second degree: customer service adds those). During the trial the owner CANNOT add any: the app/site shows "contact customer service" and a **"Call me to add my relatives"** button that files a `relative_request` in the contact inbox (one open request at a time). After the card is paid (and not expired) the owner can add relatives themselves — each is created **pending** (`added_by = member`), never active, until an admin approves. The `/family`, `/family/members` and `/family/children` endpoints keep their shape; a relative's `id` there is now its membership id, and a child sent from the app needs one of `date_of_birth` / `phone` / `national_id` too. The admin members list shows both kinds (`member_type` filter, `primary` | `relative`); counts, payments and exports are over primaries.
8. **Admin must hear about pending work.** A member-added relative also files a `pending_relative` inbox message; the admin sees banners + sidebar badges (`AdminAlerts`), a **"Pending relatives"** filter and a **"Trial"** filter on the members list, and a pending-count badge per row. Manual payments wait in `/admin/subscription`. Do not add a way to create an active relative from a member-facing endpoint.
9. **Profile nudge.** While a signed-in member's profile is incomplete, the website and the app show a small notice **every 60 seconds** with the percentage (`progress.overall.percent`) and what is missing (address, job title, email, …). It also says: to add relatives contact customer service, and a card holds up to 15 first-degree relatives. It stops by itself at 100%. The figure is `RegistrationProgress` in the backend — clients never count fields.
10. **Errors are logged on both sides** (see the error-logging rule in the backend `AGENTS.md`): backend `Log::error` with context; front ends report to `/api/v1/client-errors` (the website relays through its own `POST /client-errors`).
11. **Tests that pin this:** backend `tests/Feature/TrialCardTest.php` and `PaymobPaymentTest.php`; website `tests/Feature/MemberTrialCardTest.php`. Run backend tests on a throwaway database (never the live one).

18. **Several cards per member.** Any member (primary or relative) can hold any number of EXTRA cards, `physical` or `soft`, in `member_extra_cards` (backend model `MemberExtraCard`; NOT the card-code `membership_cards`). `card_number` NULL = follows the member's own number; a value = a different number that opens the SAME member (card lookups and card-number sign-in resolve it). `status`: `requested` (ordered by the customer, waiting for staff) | `active` | `cancelled`. A physical-card order from the app or the website (item 16) files one `requested` card per copy on the member the line names (`source = order`, `order_id`); the admin adds, edits, confirms or cancels them on the member page and sees `N cards · physical · soft` (amber while any is requested) in the members list. An APPROVED physical card (status → `active`) gets a SOFT copy automatically (`parent_card_id`, same number, follows the physical card's number and cancellation); `GET /mobile/my-cards` lists every non-cancelled extra card of the member and their relatives (physical, soft, `requested` = waiting) and the app (`PhysicalCardOrder` "My cards") and the website draw it — clients never create cards. Clients only order (same number); the website page is `/account/physical-cards` (`MemberPhysicalCardController`, relays `/api/v1/mobile/physical-cards` on the member token). Tests: backend `Admin/MemberExtraCardTest` + `PhysicalCardOrderTest`, website `MemberTrialCardTest` (physical card order).

### Where it lives (app)
- `src/api/membership.ts` (status, trial, subscribe, relatives request), `components/{TrialCardForm,CardStatusBanner,SubscribePanel,ProfileReminder}.tsx`; `AccountGate` offers the trial to a signed-in member with no card; `ProfileView` shows the banner, the subscribe panel and the relatives rules; `AddMemberForm` lists first-degree relations only.
- A `REG-` membership number from the sign-in is a placeholder, NOT a card — `AccessModal` ignores it.
- Failures are reported with `utils/reportError.ts` (`POST /api/v1/client-errors`).

- **Website checkout spends points / wallet too (2026-11-14).** Same rule as the app: the signed-in member sees two switches (loyalty points, wallet), both OFF until pressed; the site sends `use_points` / `use_wallet` (default false) with the order and the backend decides. Clients draw `GET /loyalty` (`points`, `points_per_egp`, `points_max_per_order`, `wallet_balance`), each product's `points_payable` / `points_max` (guest product resources) and, from the order answer, `points_used`, `points_amount`, `points_rate` (the points-per-pound rate ARCHIVED on the order at the sale, `orders.points_rate`), `wallet_amount`, `amount_due`. The preview in the cart only mirrors the backend rule. Website: `FetchMemberSpendable`, `CheckoutPointsWalletTest`.

## Multi-store orders: shipping per parcel and invoices (2026-11-19)
- The backend splits an order into PARCELS (one store, several stores sharing a warehouse, or the whole order when the admin set "one shipping price") and prices each parcel's delivery. The app never computes it: the cart/checkout asks `POST /mobile/orders/quote` (`quoteOrder`, debounced, re-asked when the governorate, basket or card changes) and draws `delivery_total` plus one line per parcel when there are several (with `remaining_for_free` per parcel). If the quote fails the old estimate stays and the error is reported (`reportClientError`).
- Order answers carry `shipments[]` and `invoice_all_url`; `OrdersPage` draws the parcels and opens the backend's printable invoice (`invoiceLink(url, isAr)` → `?lang=`) per parcel or for the whole order. `shipments` empty = an old order, drawn as before. Test: `src/test/multiStoreShipping.test.tsx`.

**Sharing loyalty points is visible in three places (2026-11-19).** `TransfersPanel` takes `kindOnly="points"`, `initialWho` (a card/phone to start from), `withLog` and `noteWhenOff` (a friendly note when `GET /transfers` says the feature or the points kind is off — never hard-coded). It is offered from the points guide (`/quiz/points`, «مشاركة نقاط الولاء» button), on every relative / owner row of the family list (pre-filled with that person's CARD NUMBER; hidden for yourself and when there is no number) and on its own page `/account/share` (`PointsSharing`, account list entry «مشاركة نقاط الولاء») with the log of sent / received / declined / cancelled / expired. Test: `src/test/pointsSharing.test.tsx`.

**Hiding an order (2026-11-19).** An order still untouched (nothing paid, approved or shipped) carries `can_hide`; the buyer may hide it after a confirm dialog (`POST /mobile/orders/{code}/hide {confirm:true}`, `hideOrder()` in `src/api/orders.ts`). Nothing is deleted: the order drops out of their lists (`hidden_by_buyer`, `forgetOrder()` removes the local snapshot) and the admin still sees it flagged. The app never decides eligibility; a 422 shows the backend message and reloads the order. Test `src/test/hideOrder.test.tsx`.

- **Sharing the referral code refills the quiz plays (2026-11-19).** The backend (`QuizShareRefills`, `POST /referral/shared`) gives +1 play on EVERY section for today per accepted share, at most `quiz_share_refills_per_day` a day (admin setting, default 1, 0 = off); a share cannot be verified, so that cap is the anti-farming rule. The app calls it from `/account/invite` (share sheet, WhatsApp, copy) via `utils/referralShare.ts` — fire and forget, signed-in only — and stores today's bonus (`share_bonus_plays_today`, also read from `GET /loyalty` and the upload reply) in `utils/quizLimit.ts`: a section allows `PLAYS_PER_DAY + bonus` plays, the bonus resets at midnight with the plays. The used-up panel offers «شارك كود الإحالة…» while refills are left. Points booking is unchanged: the backend raises its own daily cap by the same bonus, so normal and extra plays pay normally. Tests: `src/test/quizLimit.test.ts`, `referralShareRefill.test.ts`.
- **Share-to-unlock buttons (2026-11-19).** `components/ShareUnlock.tsx` («شارك كود الإحالة وافتح المحاولات تاني») goes straight to `/account/invite`; it is drawn in the quiz on the used-up panels, the end-of-play screen, a banner at the top of the sections list while ANY section is used up, and under each used-up section card. Signed-out: nothing; `share_refills_left_today` = 0: a polite "already used today" note instead. After a granted share the invite screen offers «ارجع للعبة». Test: `src/test/shareUnlock.test.tsx`.
- **Quiz refills show up on their own (2026-11-19):** `QuizRefillWatcher` (on the sections list and inside the used-up panel) re-reads `GET /loyalty` when it opens, when the app returns to the foreground and every 60 s while it is on screen (`refreshLoyalty` skips offline / signed out and shares one in-flight request). When the stored `share_bonus_plays_today` goes UP after it was already known today (`onRefillGranted` in `quizLimit.ts`; the first value of the day is a load, not news) the sections open at once and a short notice shows («حد سجّل بكودك»). Test: `src/test/quizRefillWatcher.test.tsx`.
