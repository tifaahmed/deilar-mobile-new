# CLAUDE.md — Deilar mobile app (`deilar-mobile-new`)

Read `CLAUDE_GUIDE.md` for the architecture. This app is a CLIENT of `../ibusiness_medical` (all rules and numbers live there) and shares its product contract with `../deilar` (the website).

## UI rules (every screen)

- **A field being typed in is never hidden behind the keyboard.** One global rule, `src/utils/keyboardGuard.ts` (installed in `main.tsx`): a focused text field is scrolled to the middle of the visible area once the keyboard is up, and again when it changes size — in the page and inside modals / sheets. Do NOT add per-form scroll code, and do not replace it; a new form needs nothing. Test: `src/test/keyboardGuard.test.ts`.

## Trial cards, subscriptions & relatives — THE CONTRACT (same text in all three projects)

> Deilar is three projects: `ibusiness_medical` (Laravel backend — owns every rule and every number), `deilar` (the website) and `deilar-mobile-new` (the app). The website and the app are CLIENTS: they relay what a member typed and DRAW what the backend answers. If you change a rule, change it in `ibusiness_medical` first and update this section in all three files in the same change.

1. **Free trial card.** A member makes one from the app or the website with ONLY: the phone they signed in with (proved by the SMS code), a **name** (no national ID). It works immediately, `plan = trial`, and expires after `membership_trial_months` (default **1 month**; admin setting). It holds the OWNER ONLY — never a relative.
2. **One trial per phone, ever.** Enforced by the backend: `trial_claims` (DB unique index on `phone` — survives deleted cards) and any other account with the same phone that holds a card. The national ID is NOT asked for any more (it can be added later in the profile). A second device signs into the SAME account (login is by phone) so it just sees the same card; another account row with the same phone is refused (`reason`: `phone_taken`, `already_has_card`). Never weaken this, never add a client-side "retry with another phone".
3. **The backend decides what a card IS.** `Membership::state()` → `pending | trial | trial_expired | active | expired | inactive`; clients get it as `status` (`MembershipStatus::forCard` for anyone scanning, `forMember` for the signed-in owner). Clients NEVER compute expiry, price, "can add relatives" or the profile percentage themselves.
4. **Expired card = a big RED sign.** Whenever a card is scanned or opened (QR page, the website's `/membership/{number}`, the app's card screen) and `status.expired` is true (a trial that ended OR a paid card that lapsed) the page leads with a large red "CARD EXPIRED" sign and three options: **Subscribe now with Paymob** (only if `status.can_pay_online`; hidden when the price is 0 or Paymob is unset), **Call customer service** (`tel:` the phone), **Message customer service** (WhatsApp). Contacts come from `status.contact` (admin settings) — never hard-code a number. A running trial shows a small amber banner with the days left. An expired card earns NO member price (`Membership::isCurrent()`).
5. **Paying for a card.** Paymob (online, automatic — the callback activates the card), or through customer services: **cash on delivery**, **Vodafone Cash**, **Instapay** (the last two with a receipt upload). Two plans, both admin settings: yearly (`membership_price`, `membership_months`) and half-year (`membership_half_price`, `membership_half_months`); a price of 0 = that plan is not sold. Clients draw `status.plans` (`year`/`half`, each with price and months) and send the chosen one as `plan`; the backend refuses a plan that is not on sale and charges ITS price (`MembershipPlan::find`). Every accepted payment writes a `member_payments` row (`ActivateSubscription`), with the plan's months. A receipt is a CLAIM: it never extends a card by itself — an admin confirms it at `/admin/subscription`. Only `ActivateSubscription` adds card time (a verified Paymob callback, or an admin pressing confirm); it is idempotent, so a repeated callback or click can never grant two periods. A trial becomes `full` and is paid from the day of payment. **Which methods are SHOWN is an admin switch** (`/admin/setting`, `App\Support\PaymentMethods`): `payment_paymob_card_enabled` and `payment_paymob_wallet_enabled` (Paymob by bank card / by mobile wallet such as Vodafone Cash — two separate buttons, each its own Paymob integration: `PAYMOB_CARD_INTEGRATION_ID`, `PAYMOB_WALLET_INTEGRATION_ID`; clients send `paymob_channel` = `card`|`wallet`; both default on), `payment_cod_enabled` (cash on delivery, default off), `payment_wallet_enabled` (wallet transfer / Vodafone Cash with receipt, default off), `payment_instapay_enabled` (default off). Orders and subscriptions are both gated by it and the backend refuses a hidden method; clients draw only `payment_methods` (`/mobile/shop`, `/partner/shop-settings`) and `status.payment_methods` — never a hard-coded list.
6. **Paymob callbacks** are public and trusted ONLY through the HMAC; a paid amount that is not the price is never accepted; every HTTP exchange is stored verbatim with timing in `order_payment_logs` (shared with orders). Subscription references are `SUB<id>A<attempt>`, order codes are `DL-XXXXXXXX` — never mix them.
7. **Relatives are MEMBERS (one table, not two).** A relative is a `memberships` row with `member_type = 'relative'`, `parent_membership_id` (the PRIMARY card — never another relative) and `relationship`, plus its own `users` row (name, phone, date of birth live there, as for any member). The old `family_members` table is RETIRED: its rows were copied by migration `2026_10_29_100000_add_relatives_to_memberships` (`legacy_family_member_id` keeps the link; a phone / e-mail / National ID already held by someone else was NOT copied and sits in `legacy_notes`, the closest primary keeps it). The table itself is dropped in a LATER release, after the copy is confirmed in production. Rules (model `Membership::guardRelative` + `App\Actions\Membership\{CreateRelative,UpdateRelative}` — the only way to make or edit one): a relative needs a relation and a primary parent; **at least one of phone / date of birth / National ID** (all three allowed; rows copied from the old table are exempt); phone, e-mail and National ID are unique like any member's; **no subscription of its own** (`plan = 'relative'`, no expiry — `state()`/`isCurrent()` follow the primary); address, partner, company and sales are inherited from the primary; the number is six digits like every new member (`Membership::newNumber()`; older numbers are untouched); a **son or daughter may be typed with the FIRST name only** (the primary's name is appended), every other relation needs the full name; **deleting a primary deletes its relatives**; a relative signs in only with THEIR OWN card number (`CardLoginController`; the code goes to the phone on that card, or they add their own once) — phone sign-in (`MemberOtp::findMember`) stays primary-only. A card holds at most **15 first-degree relatives** (wife, husband, son, daughter, father, mother — siblings are second degree: customer service adds those). During the trial the owner CANNOT add any: the app/site shows "contact customer service" and a **"Call me to add my relatives"** button that files a `relative_request` in the contact inbox (one open request at a time). After the card is paid (and not expired) the owner can add relatives themselves — each is created **pending** (`added_by = member`), never active, until an admin approves. The `/family`, `/family/members` and `/family/children` endpoints keep their shape; a relative's `id` there is now its membership id, and a child sent from the app needs one of `date_of_birth` / `phone` / `national_id` too. The admin members list shows both kinds (`member_type` filter, `primary` | `relative`); counts, payments and exports are over primaries.
8. **Admin must hear about pending work.** A member-added relative also files a `pending_relative` inbox message; the admin sees banners + sidebar badges (`AdminAlerts`), a **"Pending relatives"** filter and a **"Trial"** filter on the members list, and a pending-count badge per row. Manual payments wait in `/admin/subscription`. Do not add a way to create an active relative from a member-facing endpoint.
9. **Profile nudge.** While a signed-in member's profile is incomplete, the website and the app show a small notice **every 60 seconds** with the percentage (`progress.overall.percent`) and what is missing (address, job title, email, …). It also says: to add relatives contact customer service, and a card holds up to 15 first-degree relatives. It stops by itself at 100%. The figure is `RegistrationProgress` in the backend — clients never count fields.
10. **Errors are logged on both sides** (see the error-logging rule in the backend `AGENTS.md`): backend `Log::error` with context; front ends report to `/api/v1/client-errors` (the website relays through its own `POST /client-errors`).
11. **Tests that pin this:** backend `tests/Feature/TrialCardTest.php` and `PaymobPaymentTest.php`; website `tests/Feature/MemberTrialCardTest.php`. Run backend tests on a throwaway database (never the live one).
12. **Guest → trial flow (app and website alike).** A visitor leaves name + phone in the card popup → the client asks the backend `phone-check` (`has_card` only, NO SMS; the website uses the key-gated `partner/auth/phone-check`, the app `mobile/auth/phone-check`) → a phone with no card is offered the free card → confirm name + phone → OTP → **gender** → `POST /membership/trial` (`gender` male|female; optional on the backend, required by both UIs). Registering at the code leaves an inert `REG-` row; the card goes active only when the trial form is submitted. The trial (amber) and expired (red, "no discount, full price") reminders repeat every 60 s and lead to payment. Admin: the members list shows a Trial badge with a ✕ to remove it by hand, a *Paid from the app* filter and per-method filters (Paymob / Vodafone Cash / Instapay / cash), and the show page lists every subscription payment with its receipts; confirming a payment removes the trial automatically (`ActivateSubscription`). Payments record `channel` (`mobile`/`website`).
13. **Loyalty points from the app's quiz game.** The app's kids' quiz (`/quiz`) gives **+10 loyalty points per right answer and −10 per wrong one or a missed 20-second clock; the balance may go negative.** The backend owns the numbers (`config/loyalty.php`, `loyalty_transactions` — append-only, balance = SUM) and decides the points from `correct`; a client never sends points. The app plays fully offline: every answer is written to the phone first (`utils/quizStore.ts`) and uploaded in the background to `POST /api/v1/loyalty/quiz-answers` (token; batches ≤ 100; each answer has a client-made `id`, unique per member, so a repeated upload books once) whenever the app opens, the connection returns or a member signs in; `GET /api/v1/loyalty` returns the balance. A player with no card sees, after the game, why to fill in their data (create the free trial card) to keep the points. The app mirrors the ±10 figure (`QUIZ_POINTS`) only to show it offline — change both together. **Sections and the daily allowance:** the quiz has several sections (today football `mcq:football`, puzzles `mcq:fawazeer`, general knowledge `mcq:general` and history `mcq:history`; each is a questions file in `src/data`, a pictures folder in `src/assets`, a line in `src/data/quizSections.ts` and its key in `sections` of the backend's `config/loyalty.php`). **Each section allows 5 plays a day** (`plays_per_day`, app `PLAYS_PER_DAY`): three sections allow 15, five each. The app counts a play when it starts, the day ends at midnight on the phone, and a used-up section shows a countdown to midnight plus a "you can play again" notice (in-app banner, and a system notice when the player allowed notifications). The backend enforces the same cap on points: answers of a further play that day are not booked (`over_daily_limit` in the upload reply). **Each play is 10 questions drawn easiest → hardest** (`difficulty` 1–10 on every question in `src/data/footballQuiz.json`; never-met questions first; a stage ends when every question was answered right once, then the next stage starts with all of them). **Badges are admin data** (`/admin/loyalty`, table `quiz_badge_definitions`: kind `solved` = right answers, `streak` = right in a row, `stage` = stage reached; threshold, title, icon, reward points, on/off; `config/loyalty.php` only seeds them). Earning one books its reward in the ledger once (`loyalty_transactions.source = badge`); the backend works it out from the booked answers, never from a client claim. An admin can give or take back points by hand to any member (`source = admin`, with a note and the admin's id); nothing in the ledger is edited or deleted. `GET /api/v1/loyalty` returns the balance and the badge catalog, `GET /api/v1/loyalty/history` returns plays, badges (with their reward) and admin grants in one newest-first sequence, which the app draws as the player's log. Tests: backend `tests/Feature/QuizLoyaltyTest.php` and `AdminQuizLoyaltyTest.php`, app `src/test/{quizStore,quizData,quizPick}.test.ts`.

14. **Card-number sign-in.** A card number alone opens NOTHING. The app first asks `POST /mobile/auth/card/phone-hint` (NO SMS; only the masked phone, i.e. its last 4 digits) and shows it so the holder can check it is theirs; only after they confirm does it ask `POST /mobile/auth/card/otp` and the code goes to the phone ON the card (only the masked number comes back); `.../card/otp/verify` signs in. A card with no phone answers `has_phone: false` and sends nothing: the app keeps only `pendingCard` (never `cardNumber`), shows an amber "add your phone" notice every 60 s, and the holder may ADD a phone (`.../card/phone` → code to the new number → `.../card/phone/verify` saves it and signs in). A phone already on a card is NEVER changed, and a phone owned by another account is refused (`phone_taken`). Backend: `Mobile\CardLoginController`, test `CardNumberLoginTest`; app: `AccessModal` (`card`/`addphone` steps), `AddPhoneReminder`. Mirror this in the website and backend contract text.

15. **Verified phones.** `users.phone_verified_at` (null = not verified). A phone becomes verified ONLY when a code that really travelled by SMS to it is typed back (`MemberOtp::verify` → `confirmPhone`; a fixed/admin code proves nothing about the number). Every member who was not a relative was already checked with the customer, so migration `2026_10_29_160000_add_phone_verified_at_to_users` marks them verified. A relative's phone typed in by the primary member (`POST /family/children`, `/family/members`) is saved UNVERIFIED with no SMS; `GET /family`, `/family/overview` return `phone_verified` and the app draws a "phone not verified" tag. Mirror this in the website and backend contract text.

16. **Physical cards.** A PRIMARY member whose card is PAID and current (yearly or half-year; never a trial, an expired card or a relative) orders plastic cards from `/card/physical` for themselves and their approved relatives (the same number may be bought several times, up to `max_per_card` per number). Prices are admin settings `physical_card_price` (per card, default 50) and `physical_card_shipping_price` (per order, default 50); the client draws `GET /mobile/physical-cards` (`eligible`, `reason`, prices, `cards`, `payment_methods`) and NEVER decides who may order or what it costs. `POST /mobile/physical-cards/orders` makes an ordinary `orders` row (`source = physical_card`, lines named by card number, no `product_id`), paid with the methods the admin switched on exactly like a shop order (Paymob via `/mobile/orders/{code}/pay`, wallet transfer with a receipt, cash on delivery); the admin orders list marks each order **Products** or **Physical cards** and filters by `kind`. Tests: backend `PhysicalCardOrderTest`, app `src/test/physicalCardOrder.test.tsx`. Mirror this in the website and backend contract text.

17. **Payment methods are switched PER PLACE.** Each method (Paymob card, Paymob wallet, cash on delivery, wallet transfer / Vodafone Cash, Instapay) has its main switch AND one switch per context — `products` (shop), `cards` (physical card orders), `membership` (buying a subscription) — `payment_<context>_<method>_enabled` in `/admin/setting`, all on by default. A method shows in a context only when both are on (`PaymentMethods::…($context)`); the backend refuses a method hidden in that context (orders, physical cards, subscriptions, and `POST /orders/{code}/pay` by the order's own context). Clients draw only their own list: `payment_methods` of `/mobile/shop` (products), of `/mobile/physical-cards` (cards) and `status.payment_methods` / `online_channels` (membership) — never a shared or hard-coded list. Test: backend `PaymentMethodsPerContextTest`. Mirror this in the website and backend contract text.

### Where it lives (app)
- `src/api/membership.ts` (status, trial, subscribe, relatives request), `components/{TrialCardForm,CardStatusBanner,SubscribePanel,ProfileReminder}.tsx`; `AccountGate` offers the trial to a signed-in member with no card; `ProfileView` shows the banner, the subscribe panel and the relatives rules; `AddMemberForm` lists first-degree relations only.
- A `REG-` membership number from the sign-in is a placeholder, NOT a card — `AccessModal` ignores it.
- Failures are reported with `utils/reportError.ts` (`POST /api/v1/client-errors`).
- Guest → trial flow (`AccessModal`): visitor name + phone → `POST /mobile/auth/phone-check` (`has_card`, no SMS) → a number without a card is offered the free card (`offer` → `confirm` → OTP `code` → the popup closes and the **/card page** (`TrialCardForm`: first + second name, phone, gender → `startTrial`) makes the card; there is no details form inside the popup). Registering at OTP verify leaves an inert `REG-` row; the card goes active only when `TrialCardForm` is submitted. `MembershipReminder` repeats the trial (amber) / expired (red, no discounts) notice every 60 s and leads to the profile's subscribe panel. `gender` is sent to `POST /membership/trial` (optional on the backend, required by the app UI).
- Area → card data: `SaveAreaPrompt` offers a signed-in member whose card has no governorate/city to save the area picked on the device (`AreaModal` → `utils/area.ts`) via `PUT /profile` (`saveMemberArea`); "Not now" is remembered per area. The street address is still typed in the profile.
- App tests: `npm test` (vitest + Testing Library, `src/test/`): guest→trial flow, reminders, save-area prompt. Backend counterpart: `tests/Feature/GuestTrialJourneyTest.php`.
